### What the script does
1. Initialises `OpenAI` and `Composio` with `OpenAIProvider`, binding the entity `zealt-user01`.
2. Loads three Composio tools in OpenAI function-calling format:
   - `SLACK_FIND_CHANNELS` – discovers the `#general` channel ID
   - `SLACK_FETCH_CONVERSATION_HISTORY` – retrieves the last 5 messages
   - `SLACK_CHAT_POST_MESSAGE` – posts the summary back to the channel
3. Runs an **agentic loop** — the GPT-4o model decides which tools to call; the loop executes them via `composio.provider.handle_tool_calls()` and feeds results back until the model produces a final text response.
4. Saves the summary paragraph to `/home/user/summary.txt`.
